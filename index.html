Who I Am

About Me

Turning complex spatial data into actionable insights for municipal planning, water resources, and urban infrastructure

I am a Geospatial Analyst and GIS Specialist based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, holding an M.Sc. in Geoinformatics and a B.Sc. in Botany. Over the past 3+ years, I have specialized in catchment delineation, drainage and stream order analysis, municipal flood inundation mapping, satellite remote sensing, drone and cadastral survey integration, and custom Python automation.

Currently at Atomep Enteam Pvt. Ltd., I deliver GIS outputs for major municipal and government assignments including AMC, GMC, SMC, and GUDM, as well as extensive private corporate and industrial master plans. My work focuses on core GIS workflows: converting client-provided CAD drawings and plot schemes into georeferenced GIS vectors, developing them into high-resolution proposed grading raster surfaces, delineating catchments in QGIS & ArcMap, performing drainage stream ordering, preparing DEMs for hydraulic workflows (using HEC-RAS basics), cartographically mapping model outputs, and maintaining asset inventories of over 800 structures.

"As a GIS Specialist, I don't just execute spatial workflows — I build customized PyQGIS tools and automated pipelines that streamline catchment delineation, DEM modification, and publication-ready cartography."

Previously at Agriculture Insurance Company of India (AICL), I integrated cadastral records, administrative units, and multi-temporal satellite imagery for insurance unit mapping and crop loss assessment. My academic roots in Botany give me an authentic ecological understanding for LULC, NDVI, and climate-smart agricultural analysis.

Custom GIS Tools & Scripts

Proprietary PyQGIS plugins and geospatial automation tools built to accelerate engineering pipelines

01. Raster Post-Development Grading Engine

Develops proposed elevation raster surfaces for plots, boundary walls, and internal roads, generating graded terrain models directly from layout attributes or custom elevation inputs.

Terrain Grading Tool

02. Trapezoidal Channel DEM Designer

Modifies and burns 3D trapezoidal channels directly into DEM rasters for hydraulic modeling, built with HEC-RAS style "Dig Only" (min elevation) logic and cKDTree orthogonal projection.

PyQGIS DEM Tool

03. DEM & Elevation Mosaic Engine

Seamlessly merges and mosaics adjacent or overlapping DEM and elevation raster tiles directly in QGIS, eliminating edge boundary steps and producing unified elevation surfaces.

Elevation Processing

04. Universal Raster Boundary Extractor

Extracts precise non-null boundary footprints and boundary polygons from any raster dataset, including drone orthophotos, DEMs, DSMs, and multi-spectral satellite imagery.

Raster Footprint Tool

05. LULC Raster-to-Vector Classifier & Styler

Converts classified LULC rasters into clean vector layers, managing and classifying all feature codes into attributes, and automatically loading the output with synchronized QGIS symbology.

LULC Conversion Tool

06. Flow Direction & Stream Order Animator

Extracts Strahler stream orders into vector attributes and generates animated GIF flow visualizations, rendering dynamic width-scaled and color-coded line pulses to present water flow direction.

QGIS Animation Tool

07. Batch SHP to KMZ with Symbology & Labels

Batch exports single or multiple Shapefiles into styled Google Earth KMZ files, preserving native QGIS categorized symbology, color schemes, opacity, and dynamic label expressions.

ETL & KMZ Pipeline

08. Universal Location Search Bar

Omni-search panel for QGIS like Google Earth Pro: parses any coordinate format (DMS, UTM, MGRS, Plus Codes, web map links), provides live geocoding (Photon, Nominatim, Esri), queries loaded layer attributes, and shows place cards with Street View links.

QGIS GUI Plugin

09. Direct Coordinate Geometry Injector

Directly injects and manages Latitude/Longitude coordinates into Point, Line, and Polygon features, enabling rapid coordinate assignment, vertex editing, and spatial attribute updating.

Vector Geometry Tool

10. Auto Cross-Section & Excel Profile Engine

Directly extracts terrain elevation profiles along cross-section cut lines from DEMs, automatically generating formatted Excel workbooks with high-clarity terrain profile graphs and station-elevation data tables.

Profile & Excel Engine
